The semantic/reference implementation remains on the `0.4.0-dev` line while the portable `.rcl` archive container remains compatible with the existing package-format boundary.

```text
manifest.json

```

v0.4 extends semantics and companion evidence/governance artifacts without adding a sixth required profile payload.

## Capability freeze boundary

The v0.4 checkpoint includes the already implemented areas summarized by the overview and roadmap, including:

- portable semantic migration and capability matching;
- Behavior Intent / Goal Semantics and alternative capability paths;
- Intent proposer, discovery, context diagnostics, approval, and revision;
- Goal Vocabulary Governance;
- lightweight Experience Store, compaction, retention, archive/prune review;
- Provenance / Privacy Governance;
- Habit history, evidence, promotion, and explicit approval;
- expression, expressive timing, recommendations, and explicit optimization approval;
- adapter conformance;
- observed/statistical/repeated-session continuity evaluation;
- observed and repeated Intent Success.

This checkpoint does **not** expand that capability surface.

## What this checkpoint does not claim

This checkpoint does not claim:

- physical robot safety certification;
- causal proof of robot purpose or subjective motivation;
- consciousness or personhood transfer;
- legal privacy compliance certification;
- encryption, PKI, or access-control infrastructure completeness;
- universal success-rate or motion-similarity thresholds;
- completion of physical Robot A → Robot B validation.

## Next major direction

The next major technical direction remains **v0.5 physical Robot A → Robot B validation**.

The intended sequence is:

```text
Robot A repeated real behavior
    ↓
semantic Experience / observations
    ↓
RCL profile + evidence
    ↓
migration to a genuinely different Robot B
    ↓
target-native execution
    ↓
Observed + Repeated Intent Success
    +
behavior / expression / timing continuity measurement
    +
physical safety and user review
```

Human-facing interaction scenarios such as handover or handshake-style behavior remain useful candidates because they can expose functional success, expression, timing, and relationship-context continuity together.
